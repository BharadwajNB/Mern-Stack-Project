COMPLAINT DESCRIPTION

Persistent noise violations have been occurring in Silent Zone B of the main library, particularly between 2:00 PM and 5:00 PM on weekdays. Groups of students have been holding conversations and phone calls in the designated quiet study area, making it impossible to concentrate. Library staff were informed on March 20th but no visible enforcement action has been taken. Multiple students in the zone have expressed similar frustration. We request stricter enforcement of the quiet zone policy and visible signage reminders.
